🤝 Unit 1: That's my kind of friend!

Vocabulary

  • Personality types and qualities → Tipos y cualidades de personalidad 🧠
  • Relationships → Relaciones 🤝
  • Likes and dislikes → Gustos y disgustos ❤️

Speaking

  • Describing personalities → Describir personalidades
  • Expressing likes and dislikes → Expresar gustos y disgustos
  • Agreeing and disagreeing → Estar de acuerdo y en desacuerdo
  • Complaining → Quejarse

Grammar

  • Relative pronouns as subjects and objects → Pronombres relativos como sujetos y objetos
  • It clauses + adverbial clauses with when → Cláusulas con “it” + cláusulas adverbiales con “when”
Relative Pronouns
As the Subject of a Clause As the Object of a Clause
I like people who/that aren't too serious. I want someone (who/that) I can have fun with.
I like people who/that have a good sense of humor. I'd like someone (who/that) I can talk to easily.
It Clauses + Adverbial Clauses with When
It Clauses Adverbial Clauses with When
I like it when my roommate cleans the apartment.
I don't mind it when a friend answers the phone at the dinner table.
I can't stand it when I'm upset and people tell me to calm down.
It makes me happy when people do nice things for no reason.
It bothers me when my doctor arrives late for an appointment.
It upsets me when a close friend forgets my birthday.

💼 Unit 2: Working 9 to 5

Vocabulary

  • Jobs → Trabajos 💼
  • Career benefits → Beneficios profesionales 🎁
  • Job skills → Habilidades laborales 🧠
  • Summer jobs → Trabajos de verano ☀️

Speaking

  • Talking about possible careers → Hablar sobre posibles carreras
  • Describing jobs → Describir trabajos
  • Deciding between two jobs → Decidir entre dos trabajos

Grammar

  • Gerund phrases as subjects and objects → Frases con gerundio como sujetos y objetos
  • Comparisons with adjectives, nouns, verbs, and past participles → Comparaciones con adjetivos, sustantivos, verbos y participios pasados
Gerund Phrases
Gerund Phrases as Subjects Gerund Phrases as Objects
Playing games all day would be lots of fun. She'd be good at testing games.
Being a flight attendant sounds exciting. He'd love being a flight attendant.
Designing clothes is not a man's job. He wouldn't like being a fashion designer.
Working as a veterinarian could be rewarding. She'd enjoy working with animals.
Comparisons
Type Expressions
With adjectives sounds more/less challenging than
is harder than
is not as hard as
With nouns has better/worse hours than
has more education than
isn't as much work as
With verbs earns more/less than
earns as much as
doesn't pay as much as
With past participles is better paid than
is as well paid as
isn't as well paid as
